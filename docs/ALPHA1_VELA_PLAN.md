# SOMADEX Alpha 1 — Vela

Production milestone after Foundation 1.0.

## Goal
Build the first complete, replayable chapter of the full game rather than another technical prototype.

## World slice
- Vela town hub
- Vela outskirts
- Resonance Route
- Whispering Grove
- Tideglass Coast
- Echo Cave
- North Gate challenge area

## Content targets
- 8–12 wild base species in the Alpha encounter pool
- starter trio and first evolutions prepared in data
- 12+ NPCs with persistent dialogue/state
- 4–6 trainer encounters
- one named rival/trainer duel
- one major Vela challenge/boss
- side quests, hidden items, healing station and route unlocks
- complete chapter loop from starter selection to leaving Vela

## Graphics replacement
- 50-species base-form portrait atlas from supplied source art
- new 24 px Vela tileset
- dedicated player and NPC sprites
- world UI/HUD replacement
- battle backgrounds and effect sprites
- remove embedded base64 monster thumbnails once atlas loader is active

## Engineering rule
Foundation 1.0 contracts stay stable. Alpha work extends content and presentation through data registries, map assets and feature modules.
